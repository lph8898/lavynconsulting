# Welcome to Lavyn Consulting

I’m **Lavinia Adams**, founder of **Lavyn Consulting**, based in Brisbane. I partner with small business owners and solo founders who want to grow deliberately — without overwhelm. My work combines focused strategy, efficient systems, and one‑to‑one coaching so you can scale with confidence and keep your business running when you step away.
